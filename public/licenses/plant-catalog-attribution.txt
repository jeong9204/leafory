Leafory selected 32 accepted species names and a subset of their synonyms,
checked on 2026-10-26. Korean search labels and short candidate notes were
written for Leafory; they are not official taxonomic names or identification
results. Source descriptions and photographs are not reproduced.

Each candidate links to its individual POWO taxon record and check date.
The source list and selection policy are documented in docs/phase-6.md.
Other authorities may use different classifications. No endorsement by Kew
is implied. Existing user-entered names are never automatically corrected.
